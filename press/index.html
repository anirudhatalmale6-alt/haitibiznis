Haitian Civil Society Delegation Advances Policy Dialogue During Second Day of the 20th UN International Human Rights Summit

Delegates coordinated by GCPD and the First Haitian American Lions Club advanced dialogue on the protection of women and girls, security and youth, and access to healthcare — at United Nations Headquarters in New York.

New York, NY August 1, 2026 2 min read
Share

New York, NY | August 1, 2026 — The Haitian Civil Society Delegation, coordinated by the Global Confederation for Promotion and Development (GCPD) and the First Haitian American Lions Club, continued its participation in the 20th United Nations International Human Rights Summit at United Nations Headquarters in New York, advancing dialogue around critical human rights and development priorities affecting Haiti.

The day began with Summit sessions focused on global human rights challenges, followed by opportunities for delegates to engage in discussions, networking, and knowledge exchange with representatives from civil society organizations and international stakeholders participating in the Summit.

A key component of the day was a special delegation policy session organized by the Haitian Civil Society Delegation. During this session, three priority policy topics were presented and debated, each introduced by one of the delegation’s designated spokespersons. The discussions addressed the protection of women and girls, security, gang violence, and the protection of youth, and access to healthcare for Haiti’s youth.

The session provided a platform for delegation members to share perspectives, examine challenges, identify opportunities for collaboration, and discuss recommendations that support Haitian-led solutions. The dialogue reflected the delegation’s commitment to elevating the voices of Haitian civil society and strengthening coordination among organizations from Haiti and the Diaspora.

The delegation’s participation in the Summit continues to highlight the importance of collective action, strategic partnerships, and sustained advocacy to advance human rights, institutional strengthening, and sustainable development for Haiti.

Three Priority Policy Topics

Presented and debated during the special delegation policy session, each introduced by a designated spokesperson.

01

Protection of Women and Girls

Advancing safeguards and recommendations to protect the rights, safety, and dignity of Haitian women and girls.

02

Security, Gang Violence & Protection of Youth

Addressing insecurity and gang violence, with a focus on shielding young people and building Haitian-led solutions.

03

Access to Healthcare for Youth

Expanding access to essential healthcare services for Haiti’s young people as a foundation for development.
